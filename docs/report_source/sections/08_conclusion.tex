\section{Conclusions and Future Work}
Our manual-push apparatus records long-exposure images, depth and monitor-command histories for axial motion reconstruction. Across six independent coded exposures, measured depth gives a mean PSNR gain of 3.04\,dB over a matched endpoint-linear model. Five trials improve in both metrics. S2 loses to that model, while V3 loses to the blurred input. The controls and numerical checks support a conditional benefit from measured motion; timing, focus, pose and reference assistance still limit the result.

With two more months, we would first measure optical transitions, rolling-row timing, range origins, image scale and illumination along the path. We would then preregister selection rules and collect randomized repeated motion profiles at matched light dose. Continuous pose measurements and a depth-dependent defocus model would help separate possible causes of failure before testing optimized codes.
